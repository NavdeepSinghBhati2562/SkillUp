# Behavioral Story Bank

Aim for 10 strong stories from your 6 years. Brainstorm candidates in **Week 1**, write
stories in **Weeks 10–11**. Each should be reusable for 2–3 questions. ~2 minutes spoken.

**Format: STAR**
- **S**ituation: 1–2 sentences of context
- **T**ask: what *you* were responsible for
- **A**ction: what *you* did (say "I", not "we"), about 60% of the story
- **R**esult: a measurable outcome plus what you learned

Guides: [Tech Interview Handbook: Behavioral](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/behavioral-interview/),
[Amazon Leadership Principles](https://www.amazon.jobs/content/en/our-workplace/leadership-principles)

## Themes to cover (map each story to 1+ theme)
- [ ] Biggest technical achievement / most impactful project
- [ ] Disagreement or conflict with a teammate or manager
- [ ] A failure or mistake and what you changed afterward
- [ ] Leading without authority / influencing a decision
- [ ] Ambiguous requirements: how you created clarity
- [ ] Tight deadline / tradeoff between quality and speed
- [ ] Going beyond your scope (ownership)
- [ ] Mentoring or helping someone grow
- [ ] Learning something new fast (your Android → Flutter move, and now this full-stack switch)
- [ ] Customer or user obsession: a decision driven by user data/feedback
